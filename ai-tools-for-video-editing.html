Best AI Tools for Video Editing

AI video editing tools help creators edit footage, add captions, create short clips, improve audio, and produce engaging videos. Here are some popular tools to explore.

1. CapCut

CapCut offers video editing features such as automatic captions, effects, templates, and AI-assisted editing options. Available features may vary by device and region.

Visit CapCut

2. Descript

Descript lets creators edit video and audio using a transcript. It can be useful for interviews, tutorials, podcasts, and removing unwanted parts of recordings.

Visit Descript

3. Runway

Runway provides AI-powered video creation and editing tools. Creators can explore its features for visual effects and generative video workflows.

Visit Runway

4. VEED

VEED is a browser-based video editor with features for captions, subtitles, social videos, and AI-assisted editing.

Visit VEED

5. Canva

Canva helps users create and edit videos using templates, text, graphics, and various AI-assisted design features.

Visit Canva

How to Choose an AI Video Editor

  1. Decide whether you need short social clips or long videos.
  2. Check supported export quality and file formats.
  3. Compare free-plan limits, watermarks, and pricing.
  4. Test caption accuracy and editing controls.
  5. Review privacy terms before uploading sensitive footage.
Tip: Free features and subscription plans can change. Check each tool's official website for current details.

Conclusion

The best video editing tool depends on your project and skill level. Try a few options and choose the one that fits your workflow, budget, and publishing needs.
