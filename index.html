Helpful tips for choosing AI tools

Best Free AI Tools for Students

Students can use AI assistants to understand difficult topics, summarize notes, practice writing and organize study tasks. Always verify important facts and follow your school's rules for AI use.

AI Tools for Lead Generation

Sales and marketing teams can use research assistants, business databases and email verification tools to find relevant prospects. Check data accuracy, follow privacy laws and verify contacts before outreach.

AI Tools for Video Editing

AI video tools can help create subtitles, remove pauses, generate clips and produce voiceovers. Compare export limits, watermark policies and pricing before choosing a tool.

How to Choose an AI Writing Tool

Compare writing quality, supported languages, privacy settings and free-plan limits. Test the same prompt in different tools and review the output for accuracy before publishing.
